# v0.9.2

## Changes

- Increased vegetation risk sensitivity for specific trees farther along the visible track corridor.
- Added sparse-frame sampling guidance and a conservative medium-over-low tie-break when safe catenary clearance cannot be confirmed.
- Kept background hills, continuous forest, and unrelated green scenery excluded from obstruction evidence.
- Deduplicated evaluations by video and timestamp, retaining the more conservative assessment when duplicate records are present.
- Grouped report frames by OCR-estimated railway section and listed each unique frame chronologically in a vertical layout.
- Added continuation labels for multi-page sections and matching section headers in the detail sidebar.
